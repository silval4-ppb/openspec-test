# Purpose

Tip Status identifies the status of a tip for feed filtering.

The feed-filters proposal names two tip statuses:
- `COPYABLE`: the tip can be copied (added to the betslip) by other users.
- `INACTIVE`: the tip can no longer be copied. This could be for many reasons, one of them is that the underlying bet has been settled (todo: add other reasons).
