# Project Knowledge

This OKF bundle contains durable, human- and agent-readable knowledge.

## Areas

* [Capabilities](capabilities/) - Product capabilities and domain concepts.
* [Architecture](architecture/) - System structure and technical boundaries.
* [Decisions](decisions/) - Decisions with lasting project context.
* [Initiatives](initiatives/) - Multi-change efforts, dependencies, and project-level context.

OpenSpec remains the source of truth for normative requirements and proposed or in-progress changes. Use this bundle for explanatory knowledge, and link between the two when useful.
