# Proposal

## Why

To make the Feed more engaging, add some badges related to the tipster in its Tips that are on the Feed.

## What Changes

Add the capability to show a new badge in the top of the Tip Card.

For now, we will only support a badge that shows if the tipster is in the top X% Win Rate of all tipsters.

## Open Questions

Should these badges appear only on The Feed or in the Profile View as well?

Assuming both for now
